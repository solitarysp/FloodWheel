# FloodWheel — User Personas: Camera Map Visualization

## Persona 1: Hoang — Motorbike Commuter & Delivery Driver
- **Profile**: Rides daily across Binh Thanh, District 1, and Thu Duc. Needs quick visual checks on road water levels before traveling.
- **Goal**: Open the map, pan directly to his commute route (e.g. Dien Bien Phu or Hang Xanh), click the camera pin on the intersection, and inspect the street snapshot in under 3 seconds.

## Persona 2: Linh — Disaster Response & Fleet Manager
- **Profile**: Dispatches emergency teams and delivery vans during monsoon storms.
- **Goal**: Zoom out to see which districts have online CCTV surveillance, filter cameras by district on the map, and run instant AI flood depth analyses on critical low-lying bridges and tunnels.
