# FloodWheel — User Personas

## Persona 1: Minh — Urban Commuter & Motorcyclist
- **Role**: Daily commuter in District 1 and Binh Thanh.
- **Pain Point**: Frequently checks CCTV cameras during rainstorms before heading home. Frustrated when camera popups show gray "IMAGE NOT AVAILABLE" banners, forcing him to guess flood levels.
- **Goal**: Always see the latest real view of road conditions, even if a camera temporarily loses communication with the city server.

## Persona 2: Thao — Flood Emergency Dispatcher
- **Role**: City operations monitor tracking flooded intersections.
- **Pain Point**: Spikes in concurrent map users during flash floods slow down external municipal endpoints, causing intermittent dropouts and 404s.
- **Goal**: High-speed, resilient CCTV frame delivery with immediate cached disk fallback.
